## AVL Trees
Things I'll ask you to do:
* determine if a given tree is balanced (balance factors between -1 and 1)
* insert a node into a balanced tree and determine if there should be rotations
* perform the rotation(s) required to insert a new value into the tree
  * most points for an insertion requiring a single rotation
  * full credit for an insertion requiring two rotations

### AVL Tree Insertions
* two symmetric cases: insert into left or inserting into right
  * each of these has two symmetric cases: inserting left or right
* in total, actually have 4 different rotation(s) to worry about, but they are symmetric/mirror images
  of one another
