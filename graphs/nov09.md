## Graphs
* input/output diagrams showing relationship between variables is what an accountant would think
  of when they talk of graphs
* for computer scientists, we think of graphs as collections of nodes that are connected through
  edges
  * semi-formally, a graph G = (V, E) consists of n vertices (V) and m edges (E), where each edge
    connects one or more vertices
  * nodes and edges can have associated weights/costs depending upon the problem

### Example: Trip Planning
* Vertices: places to visit
* Edges: bus routes (along with cost of trip)
